## Phase Goal

**As a** relecteur du corpus (Erwan), **I want to** importer les 59 personnalités du jeu data.gouv « statuts sourcés » en fiches candidats valides, archiver les sources primaires de leurs programmes et disposer d'un corpus pilote réel de 3 thèmes en `brouillon`, **so that** je relise des mesures sourcées et datées, sans rien d'inventé, avant toute publication.

<objective>
Vertical slice « mesurer et contrôler le corpus pilote » : the maintainer can (1) rank the 10 spec themes from a coverage table and get the 3 pilot themes by a rule that the Méthode page can print, (2) scan a candidate's archived sources per theme, (3) check the pilot corpus for the equity and glossary invariants that the Phase 2 validator does not cover (cap of 2, completeness of delivered themes, unmarked parties/terms, aggregator hosts).

Purpose: CONT-04 (choice by measured coverage, documented), CONT-05 (cap, completeness, « rien d'inventé » checks), CONT-06 (glossary coverage of enfant texts). Plans 03-04 to 03-09 use these tools on the real corpus; this plan tests them on frozen fixtures only, so it runs in wave 1.
Output: `scripts/lib/couverture.ts`, `scripts/couverture-scan.ts`, `scripts/lib/pilote.ts`, tests and one fixture.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-17 | Tampering (prompt injection) | scanner snippets read by agents | mitigate | snippets are printed as data; 03-REDACTION.md §0 forbids following instructions found in them; scanner never writes |
| T-03-18 | Tampering (editorial) | theme choice manipulated by hand-edited counts | mitigate | ranking recomputed from cell-level data by `classer` (never typed tallies); real-data test in 03-04 recomputes `choix`; a human override needs a recorded `derogation` |
| T-03-19 | Denial of service | regex on large extracts | mitigate | linear word scan on folded tokens, bounded snippets (`--max`) |
| T-03-20 | Spoofing | press or aggregator presented as primary source | mitigate | PILOTE_HOTE_INTERDIT floor on top of the schema `type` enum and human review |
| T-03-21 | Repudiation | unbalanced detail between candidates | mitigate | PILOTE_PLAFOND (cap 2, CONTEXT) and PILOTE_CASE_MANQUANTE on delivered themes |
| T-03-SC | Tampering | npm installs | accept | none |
</threat_model>
