## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-08-14 | Denial of service | installer restarting or reloading Caddy | mitigate | no docker, caddy or systemctl call in the script (static test); recreate and reload are separate approved commands |
| T-08-15 | Denial of service | Caddyfile replaced by rename (stale bind mount) | mitigate | append with `>>`, inode checked before and after, exit 5 on change |
| T-08-16 | Information disclosure | hash or ping URL printed in logs or conversation | mitigate | values read from stdin, never echoed; tests grep every output |
| T-08-17 | Elevation of privilege | deploy user rewriting its own restriction | mitigate | `.ssh` and authorized_keys root-owned (0755/0644), single restrict,command line, no sudo or docker group |
| T-08-18 | Tampering | other `.env` lines (Vaultwarden) altered | mitigate | only the two RTP keys replaced, other lines byte-identical (tested), mode 0600 |
| T-08-19 | Denial of service | essai block with a provisional domain triggering ACME loops | mitigate | site-essai refuses reserved TLDs (exit 6) |
| T-08-20 | Tampering | override altering the original service | mitigate | override limited to volumes, environment, logging (test refuses other keys) |
</threat_model>
